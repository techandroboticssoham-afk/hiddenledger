# 🎬 Hidden Ledger

> **Hidden Stories. Billion Dollar Secrets.**

Hidden Ledger is a cinematic documentary brand that uncovers the hidden strategies, business models, engineering, psychology, and systems behind the world's biggest companies.

Every episode combines deep research, factual storytelling, AI-powered visuals, and premium documentary filmmaking to explain **why successful companies work the way they do**.

---

## 🌍 About

Most people see products.

We investigate the systems behind them.

Hidden Ledger explores questions such as:

* Why does IKEA guide customers through its showroom?
* How does McDonald's serve millions of customers every day?
* Why do companies make the design decisions they do?
* What engineering, psychology, and logistics operate behind the scenes?

Every investigation is built around verified information and presented in a cinematic documentary style.

## ⚖️ Research Philosophy

Hidden Ledger aims to present information responsibly.

Every episode is developed using publicly available information, industry research, official sources where applicable, and careful fact-checking.

When discussing business strategies or design choices, the goal is to explain well-supported concepts without overstating conclusions.
